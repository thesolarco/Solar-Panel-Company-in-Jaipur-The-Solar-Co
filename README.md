## Types of Solar Systems for Homes and Businesses

Understanding the available system types can help you make an informed decision.

### On-Grid Solar Systems

On-grid systems connect to the local electricity grid. Solar panels generate electricity during daylight hours, while the grid can supply additional power when needed. Depending on the applicable regulations and net-metering arrangements, surplus electricity may be exported to the grid.

These systems can be suitable for homes, offices, shops, and other properties with a reliable grid connection.

### Off-Grid Solar Systems

Off-grid systems operate independently of the electricity grid and generally use batteries to store energy for later consumption. They can be useful in locations where grid connectivity is unavailable or unreliable.

System sizing must account for electricity demand, battery capacity, and the availability of sunlight.

### Hybrid Solar Systems

Hybrid systems combine solar generation with grid connectivity and battery storage. Depending on their configuration, they can help reduce electricity purchases while providing backup power during grid interruptions.

They are worth considering for properties that require both energy savings and additional power reliability.
